A family church

We’ve been a spiritual home to many individuals and families. We like to think of ourselves as a family and a family church.

We’re part of a bigger Reformed family of churches that trace their roots back to Scripture though the Protestant Reformation. We’re a presbyterian church in that we are led by elders (presbyters). The Christian Reformed Churches of Australia directory lists this congregation as Toowoomba CRC.

Our primary standard for faith and life is the Bible. We’re also committed to the Biblical truths expressed in the Reformed Confessions — the Heidelberg Catechism, the Belgic Confession and the Canons of Dort.

What to expect on Sunday

From the congregation’s published welcome for visitors.

Worship together

We worship as a family of God, treasuring the old and the new. We are multi-generational, and our worship services seek to contain a blend of traditional and contemporary songs, as we seek to worship the Lord with reverence and joy.

The Bible is read and taught

The Bible is our focus, as we believe it holds forth the Word of Life. Our services always contain Bible teaching, as we seek to understand God’s word more fully and apply it to our lives.

About an hour, then a cuppa

Our services generally run for between an hour and an hour and a quarter, and then we enjoy conversation over light refreshments. We typically dress neat casual.

If you cannot be there in person

For those who are unable to join us in person, we record our services and, Lord willing, they will be ready to view on our YouTube channel about one hour after each service has finished.

Recent messages include Danger Signs (Matthew 16:1–12) and Will God let them get away with it? (Romans 11:33–36), both preached by Rev. Andrew de Vries.
