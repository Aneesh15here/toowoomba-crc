Visit

You would be welcome

If you’d like to join us for worship, we’d love to welcome you as our guest! Feel free to contact us if you have any questions.

Where and when

718 Boundary Street, Glenvale QLD 4350

The carpark entry is on Boundary Street, with the entry to the auditorium on the northern side of the building.

  • Morning worship: 9:00am, then fellowship over a cuppa
  • Evening worship: 5:00pm
  • Services generally run for between an hour and an hour and a quarter
  • We typically dress neat casual

Giving

God has been so good to us as His people, and so it’s a joy and part of our worship to bring gifts and offerings to Him to support the work of the local church and the Kingdom of God.

Our preferred method is by direct deposit into the church bank account, but we also take a collection during the morning service for your regular gifts, as well as any second or special collections we might be taking up for external causes. There is a collection box available in the evening service, if you choose to give at that time.
