Come and worship

If you’d like to join us for worship on a Sunday, we’d love to welcome you! We have two services — our morning service at 9:00am, which is followed by a time of fellowship over a cuppa, and then our evening service, which begins at 5:00pm.

13 December · 4:30pm – 7:15pm

Community Christmas Carols

We warmly invite you to join us from 4.30pm at Christlife Presbyterian Church for our annual Community Christmas Carols.

Proudly presented by the Christian Reformed Church of Toowoomba, Eastgate Bible Church and Christlife West Toowoomba Presbyterian Church.

Place published on the event: 492 South Street, Glenvale (Christlife Presbyterian Church), not the Boundary Street auditorium.

Register (Facebook link from the church event page).

12 November · 7:00pm – 8:30pm

General meeting

Our General Meeting is being held on Wednesday 12th November at 7.00pm, at the church. It is an opportunity to meet as a church to discuss the 2026 budget and future plans of the church.

As we are all part of one body, it would be great to see as many members there as possible. If you are not a member, you would still be most welcome to come along and observe the proceedings.
