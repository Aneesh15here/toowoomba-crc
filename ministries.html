Ministries

Growing in faith, together

These groups are the ones described on the church’s public events pages. Meeting places are at 718 Boundary Street, Glenvale, unless a page says otherwise. Ministry groups

Every week · 9:00am – 11:00am

Women’s Bible Study

A weekly women’s bible study where we dig deeper into God’s word and enjoying a time of fellowship together. Please speak to Christina de Vries or email us if you would like more information.

Email used on the contact page: admin@toowoombacrc.com.

Every 2 weeks · 7:30pm

Men’s Reading Group

If you are not a natural reader, sometimes it’s hard to get through a book on your own. One of the ways to motivate reading and to profit more from it, is to read with others. Pastor Andrew has started a fortnightly reading group to do just that! Speak with him if you would like more information.

Every 2 weeks · 7:00pm – 8:30pm

Thursday Night Bible Study

This fortnightly study group seeks to find fellowship and encouragement around God’s word. Feel free to come and join in! See John Moes or Danie Venter if you would like to know more.

Every 2 weeks · 7:30pm – 9:00pm

Men’s Bible Study

Join the men of our congregation as we meet together weekly to encourage one another to pursue God and grow to maturity in our lives. Please contact Pastor Andrew or email us at admin@toowoombacrc.com if you’d like more information.

Every 2 weeks · 9:30am – 11:30am · $8

Coffee, Craft & Conversation

Join us fortnightly for a morning of coffee, craft and plenty of conversation and cake! There are lots of craft options. Please speak to one of the craft leaders — Marleen Ellen, Sophie Bouchier or Alix Alexander — or email admin@toowoombacrc.com for more information.

Our entry fee is $8.00 which includes all your craft supplies plus a wonderful morning tea spread and plenty of conversation! We also have a creche available for children up to the age of 5 (no school age children).

Every 2 weeks · 7:00pm – 9:00pm

GEMS & Cadets

GEMS (Girls Everywhere Meeting the Saviour) is a fortnightly program for girls, helping them to mature in Christ through fun and teaching together.

Cadets is a fortnightly program for boys, helping them to mature in Christ through fun and teaching together.

These programs currently run on the same night — please speak with either Letty or Andrew de Kroon if you would like more information.

Every 2 weeks · 7:00pm – 9:00pm

Youth Group

A time when our young people get together to study God’s word, have fun and just do life together. Please see Mekaijla Ellen for more information.

Prime Timers

A fortnightly study and fellowship for those in the prime of life! All welcome. Please speak to Bill Wiersma if you would like more information.

Every 2 weeks · 5:00pm

GROW

What is GROW? It’s an opportunity to gather together somewhat informally on a Sunday afternoon to encourage one another as we share God’s word, sing some of our favourite songs, grow in knowledge, and grow closer together through conversation afterwards.

Ordinarily someone will share a portion of God’s word, we’ll sing some of our favourite songs, and then we’ll learn from a time of teaching in partnership with Ligonier ministries.
